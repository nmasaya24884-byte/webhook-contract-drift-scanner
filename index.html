FAQ
What this scanner does—and does not do
Is my payload uploaded?
No. The scanner does not send your JSON, field names, values, or generated test to a server. Anonymous product events contain only action names.
Does a High result prove a breaking change?
No. It is a candidate based on two samples. Whether it breaks depends on your consumer code and the provider's documented contract.
Why not use a generic JSON diff?
A generic diff shows syntax. This tool classifies likely consumer impact and creates a regression test you can adapt.
