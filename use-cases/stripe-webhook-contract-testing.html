Webhook Drift Scanner

USE CASE

Review a Stripe webhook fixture change before your consumer breaks.

Compare sanitized old and new event samples locally. Find field, type, null, nesting, and array-shape changes.

Consumer-side workflow

  1. Remove secrets and personal data from both samples.
  2. Compare structure in the browser-only scanner.
  3. Review every High candidate against Stripe documentation and your handler.
  4. Adapt the generated fixture test to call your real parser.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Stripe. Samples do not replace Stripe's documentation.
