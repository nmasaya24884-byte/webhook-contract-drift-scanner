Data processing

The free scanner compares JSON and generates tests locally in your browser. It does not transmit or store your payload, field names, field values, or generated test.

Anonymous analytics

We may record page views and allow-listed product actions such as scan started, scan completed, test copied, or a pricing button clicked. These events do not include payload content. Hosting or analytics providers may process standard connection data such as IP address and user agent under their own infrastructure policies.

Do not paste secrets

Even though processing is local, remove API keys, credentials, personal data, and production secrets before using any developer tool.

Accounts and payments

This experiment does not create accounts, accept payments, connect to GitHub, or collect waitlist email addresses.

Contact

No external contact channel is enabled during the initial experiment. This notice will be updated before collecting contact information.
