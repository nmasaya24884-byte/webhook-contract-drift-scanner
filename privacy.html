Webhook Drift Scanner

PRIVACY

Payloads stay in your browser.

Effective: 1 September 2026

Data processing

The free scanner compares JSON and generates tests locally in your browser. It does not transmit or store your payload, field names, field values, or generated test.

Minimal product analytics

We use Google Analytics to record page views and an allow-list of product actions: scanner viewed, scan started, scan completed, scan error, fixture generated, test copied or downloaded, paid plan viewed, and paid or GitHub CTA clicked. Event calls contain only the action name; they do not contain payload content, JSON paths or values, fixtures, or generated tests. Google Signals, advertising personalization signals, and advertising data collection are disabled in the site configuration. Google may process standard connection and device data under its own infrastructure and privacy terms.

Do not paste secrets

Even though payload processing is local, remove API keys, credentials, personal data, and production secrets before using any developer tool.

Accounts and payments

This experiment does not create product accounts, accept payments, connect to GitHub, or collect waitlist email addresses.

Contact

No external contact channel is enabled during the initial experiment. This notice will be updated before collecting contact information.
